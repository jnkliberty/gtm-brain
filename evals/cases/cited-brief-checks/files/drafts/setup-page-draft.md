# How long does it take to set up a handoff tool with our CRM?

Most teams go live 2 to 3 weeks after the contract is signed.

Contoso seamlessly integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive, so your reps stop retyping.

In week one, you connect your CRM and map the fields. Your admin tags each place that stores handoff notes.

In week two, a small group of reps sends test handoff notes to their account owners, and your admin checks that every note arrives in the right record before anyone on the sales team relies on it.

Look no further if your reps still paste handoff notes by hand. After go-live, handoff notes reach the owner in the CRM without a copy step. Your team keeps one place for handoff notes.
