# Ideal customer profile (example)

An example ICP for the starter brain. Replace it with your own.

- **Company:** B2B, 50 to 500 employees, selling to other businesses.
- **Motion:** founder-led or early sales team; no dedicated GTM engineer yet.
- **Stack:** any CRM in place (for example HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive) plus at least one outbound or enrichment tool.
- **Fails the ICP:** consumer companies, companies under 50 employees, agencies selling GTM services.

## Target size

The filter should return 4 to 6 accounts from the example export. Set your own range: the number of accounts your team can actually work.

- Count unique companies: records that share a domain count once.
- Count every stage. The ICP measures fit; accounts sales already owns still count.
