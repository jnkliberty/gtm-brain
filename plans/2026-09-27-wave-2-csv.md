## Requirements

- **R1** `brain/tiers.md`: tier rules with IDs, read on the confirmed value first, and a rule that revenue is checked before a tier is set.
- **R2** `brain/contact-rules.md`: target functions per tier, opening pull and ceiling per tier, expansion order, keep rules, archive rules, and departed-contact handling, each with an ID.
- **R3** Synthetic vendor lists `brain/crm-export/candidates-accounts.csv` and `candidates-contacts.csv`, with planted traps listed only in `tests/planted-defects.md`.
- **R4** Proposal actions beyond field changes: create a record, archive a record, merge records, and a task for the owner, in `brain/proposals/README.md`.
- **R5** `account-build`: proposes new accounts from the vendor list that pass the ICP filter, skips or escalates matches with existing records (same domain: match; similar name with a different domain: escalate), checks revenue before tiering, and proposes a tier per `brain/tiers.md`.
- **R6** `tiered-contact-build`: for each account, counts current contacts in target functions, proposes new contacts from the vendor list up to the tier's opening pull in expansion order, counts only contacts with a valid email, escalates accounts above the ceiling instead of trimming, and lists accounts left short with what was searched.
- **R7** `contact-retention-review`: proposes keep, archive, or hold for each existing contact at an ICP account per `brain/contact-rules.md`; departed contacts get their new employer recorded and a task for the owner when one exists; duplicates get a merge proposal.
- **R8** Every skill: "Connect" section (live CRM read-only when available, otherwise the CSV export), evidence on every claim, gaps stay gaps, writes only new proposal files, never writes to a CRM.
